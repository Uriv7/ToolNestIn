# AdSense "Low Value Content" Fix Checklist

## What Google rejected and why

Google AdSense rejected toolnestin.co.in for "Low Value Content". This happens when:
1. Pages have very little readable text (tool UI is functional but not "content")
2. About/Privacy/Terms pages are thin or missing
3. The site is brand new with no traffic or domain authority
4. Pages look like they were auto-generated

## What you must do BEFORE requesting review

### Step 1 — Deploy the updated code
Push this zip to GitHub → Vercel deploys automatically.
Wait 5 minutes for deployment to complete.

### Step 2 — Get real traffic first (CRITICAL)
Google AdSense reviewers look at whether the site has genuine users.
A site with 0 sessions in the last 30 days almost always gets rejected.

Get at least 100–200 real sessions before requesting review:
- Share in 5 WhatsApp groups today
- Post on LinkedIn (personal account, not company page)
- Share in r/india, r/IndiaInvestments
- Ask 10 friends/colleagues to visit and use a tool

This takes 3–7 days minimum.

### Step 3 — Wait 2 weeks after deployment
Google needs time to re-crawl and re-index your updated pages.
The reviewer will see the OLD cached version if you request review immediately.

Check your pages are updated: google.com/search → site:toolnestin.co.in/about
If Google still shows old content, wait longer.

### Step 4 — Self-audit before review
Visit each of these pages and verify they look complete:
- [ ] toolnestin.co.in/about — should have long story, stats, featured tools
- [ ] toolnestin.co.in/privacy — should have 8+ detailed sections
- [ ] toolnestin.co.in/terms — should exist and have 14 sections
- [ ] toolnestin.co.in/contact — should have 4 contact categories
- [ ] Any tool page — should have: description, tool, expert note, FAQs, how-to, share buttons
- [ ] Homepage — should show 80+ tools in organised categories

### Step 5 — Request review
Only after steps 1-4 are complete:
1. Go to AdSense → toolnestin.co.in → Policy violations
2. Tick "I confirm that I have fixed the issues"
3. Click "Request review"
4. Wait 1–2 weeks for Google's response

## What AdSense reviewers check (in order)

1. **About page** — is there a real story? Do they know who runs this?
2. **Privacy Policy** — does it exist? Is it comprehensive?
3. **Contact page** — can users reach someone?
4. **Tool pages** — is there real explanatory content beyond just the UI?
5. **Homepage** — does the site feel like a real product?
6. **Traffic** — has anyone actually used this site?
7. **Domain age** — how old is the domain?

## Things that will get you rejected again

❌ Requesting review before getting any traffic
❌ Requesting review before Google has re-crawled updated pages
❌ Pages that are mostly blank white space
❌ Tool pages where the FAQ section has placeholder text
❌ About page shorter than 300 words
❌ Missing Privacy Policy or Terms of Service

## Timeline expectation

If rejected again, read the specific reason carefully and fix that exact issue before re-requesting.
